Building products manufacturer

Finding what drives warranty claims

A multi-plant manufacturer needed to know why warranty costs at one facility were climbing, and its data wasn't set up to say.

The challenge

Shipment records and customer-service claims lived in separate systems with incompatible order numbers, so claims couldn't be traced back to what was built and shipped.

The work

Combined shipment data with CRM claim exports and built a defect-rate model per unit shipped that worked around the system gap instead of waiting on an IT project.

The result

A KPI dashboard with monthly trends, defect breakdowns, and a finish-risk analysis that points improvement work at the products and processes behind the claims.
