How an engagement runs

Every engagement ends with a stronger team running the plant, not a dependency on a consultant.

  1. Assess fast

    Walk the floor, read the numbers, and evaluate the leadership team in the first weeks.

  2. Install the rhythm

    Daily start-ups, visual controls, and KPI reviews from the floor to the board.

  3. Fix and restructure

    Remove the waste, make the people decisions, stabilize safety, quality, and delivery.

  4. Hand off

    Select and coach the next leader, and leave the operating system with them.

Bob Mills

Bob has spent 28 years in manufacturing, the first part of it in IT leading large ERP migrations and the rest running plants. That mix means he can read a plant's numbers and its systems as well as its shop floor.

Before founding Seven Ridge Consulting in 2024, he led operations from the plant level to the multi-site executive level, at companies ranging from a Fortune 500 global manufacturer to privately held, mid-market businesses.

He has turned around struggling plants, built operating systems used by hundreds of leaders, negotiated union contracts, and led a full plant closure. Seven Ridge brings that same hands-on leadership to private equity owners.

He holds an MBA and an undergraduate degree in computer systems.
