Building an operating system that scales

As plant manager, then director of operations, then director of organizational development, Bob built the management system one level at a time.

  • Raised productivity 15% in one year at a 300-person, $180M plant with an integrated lean management system.
  • Designed a business operating system for planning, execution, measurement, and learning, and rolled out a values-based leadership model to 200 leaders in every facility.

How an engagement runs

The goal is a plant that keeps performing after Seven Ridge leaves. Every engagement is built to end with a stronger team, not a dependency.

  1. Assess fast

    Walk the floor, read the numbers, and evaluate the leadership team in the first weeks.

  2. Install the rhythm

    Daily start-ups, visual controls, and KPI reviews that connect the shop floor to the boardroom.

  3. Fix and restructure

    Remove the waste, make the people decisions, and stabilize safety, quality, and delivery.

  4. Hand off

    Select and coach the leader who owns the results next, and leave the operating system with them.

He founded Seven Ridge Consulting in 2024 to do for private equity owners what he did as an operator: stabilize plants that have lost their footing and build the leadership systems that keep them performing.

He has also led a full plant closure, start to finish, covering finance, operations, logistics, and people. That experience helps when a portfolio needs to be reshaped, not just improved.
